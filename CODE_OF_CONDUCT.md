# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want everyone who takes part — players, contributors and maintainers — to have a respectful,
harassment-free experience, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully, and give it the same way.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its players.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, the game's chat — and when
someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **ian@rippling.computer**. Every report is reviewed promptly and kept confidential.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose conduct
breaks this code, following the Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
