角頭械鬥 Turfwar 已經停服，謝謝你的興趣與遊玩。

角頭械鬥一開始是一個 vibe coding 實驗：我想看看能不能靠 AI，復刻我小時候很喜歡玩的網頁戰術射擊遊戲 BeGone。可惜 BeGone 很早就停止更新，大約 2015 年後主流瀏覽器就跑不動了，伺服器也在 2022 年完全關閉。

本遊戲的玩家規模比較小，我暫時無法找到可持續運營的方式，所以決定停止線上服務。

遊戲程式碼是開源的（github.com/madeyexz/turfwar），有興趣的話可以自己 clone 一份來開發或部署。這個網站會長期保留單機模式，歡迎隨時回來玩。

Online play in 角頭械鬥 Turf War: Taipei has stopped. Thank you for your interest, and thank you for playing.

Turf War started as a vibe coding experiment. I wanted to see if AI could help me make a new version of BeGone, a web tactical shooter that I loved as a child. BeGone stopped getting updates a long time ago. After about 2015, most browsers could not run it. Its servers closed completely in 2022.

The number of players was small, and at this time I cannot find a way to keep the game running. Because of this, I stopped online play.

The game code is open source (github.com/madeyexz/turfwar). You can clone it to develop or deploy your own copy. This website keeps single-player mode for a long time. Come back and play when you want.

向玩家學習的電腦玩家Bots that learned from players

最新版的電腦玩家，習慣是從真人玩家的對戰錄製裡量出來的：會在哪裡卡位、什麼時候衝刺、什麼時候停下來開槍、多常換方向閃躲、買什麼槍，都照著玩家的數據調整。單機模式裡的電腦玩家就是這一版。

The latest bots got their habits from recorded matches of real players. We measured where players hold position, when they sprint, when they stop to shoot, how often they change direction, and which guns they buy. Then we set the bots to do the same. The bots in single-player mode use these habits.

對戰資料集Match dataset

從 2026 年 10 月 15 日到線上對戰停止，我們在台北伺服器錄製了線上對戰（移動、瞄準、遊戲操作與對戰事件，不含聊天與呼號），用來訓練更像真人的電腦玩家。現在已經不再錄製。

From 15 October 2026 until online play stopped, we recorded online matches on the Taipei server (movement, aim, in-game controls and match events, but no chat or callsigns). We use the recordings to train bots that play more like people. We do not record matches now.

我們一共錄到 788 位玩家的對戰：122.1 小時可以用來學移動，43.5 小時可以用來學瞄準，其中 65.6 小時在去識別化的匯出資料集裡。

We recorded the matches of 788 players. We can use 122.1 hours to learn movement and 43.5 hours to learn aim. The de-identified export set has 65.6 hours.

可以授權的只有去識別化的匯出資料集：不含呼號、聊天或任何 ID，也不含有歐盟、歐洲經濟區、英國或瑞士玩家的對戰，以及選擇排除自己對戰的玩家的對戰。資料集相關洽詢：ian@rippling.computer。想刪除自己的對戰錄製，請見隱私權政策。

Only the de-identified export set is available for licensing. It contains no callsigns, chat or IDs. It does not include matches with players in the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland, or matches with players who excluded their matches. For dataset inquiries, send an email to ian@rippling.computer. To delete your match recordings, see the Privacy Policy.
