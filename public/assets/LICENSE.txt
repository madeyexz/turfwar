All models, textures and animations in this folder were converted from CC0 1.0 packs by Quaternius
(https://quaternius.com): Sci-Fi Essentials Kit, Modular Sci-Fi MegaKit, Universal Base Characters,
Universal Animation Library and Universal Animation Library 2 (free Standard editions).
CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Converted with tools/import-assets.ts (textures resized to WebP; animations meshopt-compressed).

guns.glb: twelve guns from Quaternius' "Ultimate Gun Pack" (July 2019), CC0 1.0 Universal
(https://opengameart.org/content/low-poly-guns-pack, https://quaternius.itch.io/50-lowpoly-guns):
Pistol_4, Pistol_6, SubmachineGun_1, SubmachineGun_5, Shotgun_SawedOff, Shotgun_1, AssaultRifle_5,
Bullpup_2, AssaultRifle2_2, SniperRifle_4, SniperRifle_3, Bullpup_3. Converted from OBJ with
tools/import-guns.ts (flipped to barrel -X, rescaled, meshopt-compressed).
