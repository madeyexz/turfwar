All models, textures and animations in this folder were converted from CC0 1.0 packs by Quaternius
(https://quaternius.com): Sci-Fi Essentials Kit, Modular Sci-Fi MegaKit, Universal Base Characters,
Universal Animation Library and Universal Animation Library 2 (free Standard editions).
CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Converted with tools/import-assets.ts (textures resized to WebP; animations meshopt-compressed).

guns.glb: guns and accessories from Quaternius' "Ultimate Gun Pack" (July 2019), CC0 1.0 Universal
(https://opengameart.org/content/low-poly-guns-pack, https://quaternius.itch.io/50-lowpoly-guns):
SubmachineGun_3 (MP5), SubmachineGun_2 (MP7), AssaultRifle2_1 (M4A1), AssaultRifle2_2 (M110),
AssaultRifle2_3 (M249), Shotgun_1 (M1014), Pistol_3 (M9A1), Accessories/Bayonet_2 (knife),
Accessories/Silencer_2 (suppressor) and Accessories/Grip (vertical grip). Converted from OBJ with
tools/import-guns.ts (flipped to barrel -X, rescaled, meshopt-compressed). Optics, bipods, the M249's
stock and ammo box, lasers, flashlights and other attachments are modelled in code (src/render).

sfx/: gunshots from "The Free Firearm Sound Library" (Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras and
Matthew Nanney) and reload/handling foley by SpringySpringo, BMacZero (Brian MacIntosh), zer0_sol and
LFA, all CC0 1.0 Universal on OpenGameArt. Built by tools/fetch-sounds.ts; sources and per-file
mapping in sfx/LICENSE.txt.
