All models, textures and animations in this folder were converted from CC0 1.0 packs by Quaternius
(https://quaternius.com): Sci-Fi Essentials Kit, Modular Sci-Fi MegaKit, Universal Base Characters,
Universal Animation Library and Universal Animation Library 2 (free Standard editions).
CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Converted with tools/import-assets.ts (textures resized to WebP; animations meshopt-compressed; props.glb
cut to the placed props and geometry meshopt-compressed losslessly by tools/compress-models.ts).

soldiers.glb, soldier-anims.glb: the soldier characters and their clips from Quaternius' "Ultimate Modular Men"
pack (Swat, Farmer, Punk, Worker, Adventurer, Casual_2, Casual_Hoodie; Feb 2022), CC0 1.0 Universal
(https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularcharacters.html). Bones renamed to the soldier rig's names, meshes merged
with their colours baked as vertex colours and compressed by tools/import-modular.ts.

operator.glb: the third-person soldier, from "Soldier" by Grab3D (https://grab3d.com), CC0 1.0 Universal. Its
licence file states: "AI-assisted: base mesh and textures generated with Meshy". Converted by
tools/import-operator.ts: simplified to 14 000 triangles, posed onto the skeleton of soldiers.glb (which stays in
this folder as that skeleton's source and is not loaded by the game), textures resized, and a navy copy of the
uniform painted for the SWAT side.

arsenal.glb: the store's guns and the knife, converted by tools/import-arsenal.ts (flipped to barrel -X, rescaled,
simplified, meshopt-compressed; textures packed and resized):
- Stein Games' "Free Classic Weapons Pack", CC0 1.0 Universal (stated in the pack's licence file): MP5, M4A1,
  and the AK-47, M14, MAC-10, M1911 and pump shotgun that stand in for the M249, M110, MP7, M9A1 and M1014.
- 3dmodelscc0's "Guns & Explosives" and "Melee Weapons Pack #1" (https://www.3dmodelscc0.com), CC0 1.0 Universal
  (stated on the packs' web pages; the archives hold no licence file): see tools/import-arsenal.ts for the model
  each pack gives.

guns.glb: the suppressor and vertical grip (Accessories/Silencer_2, Accessories/Grip) from Quaternius' "Ultimate
Gun Pack" (July 2019), CC0 1.0 Universal (https://opengameart.org/content/low-poly-guns-pack,
https://quaternius.itch.io/50-lowpoly-guns). Converted from OBJ with tools/import-guns.ts. Optics, bipods, lasers,
flashlights and other attachments are modelled in code (src/render).

sfx/: gunshots from "The Free Firearm Sound Library" (Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras and
Matthew Nanney) and reload/handling foley by SpringySpringo, BMacZero (Brian MacIntosh), zer0_sol and
LFA, all CC0 1.0 Universal on OpenGameArt. Built by tools/fetch-sounds.ts; sources and per-file
mapping in sfx/LICENSE.txt.

The Taipei maps (Ximending, Taipei 101 · Xinyi and Taipei 101 · 88F: shared/maps/ximending*.ts, xinyi*.ts, taipei101.ts)
and the Taipei 101 outline (tower101.ts) are this project's own layouts, drawn from public knowledge of the real
places. They are not assets in this folder and use no data from other games or map databases.
